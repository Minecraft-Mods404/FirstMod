# Super Slime Mod

**So much SLIME!** This Fabric mod turns Minecraft into a slime-powered adventure with new crafting materials, durable slime gear, explosive weapons, boss slimes, potions, a slime-themed dimension, and a spawner that produces slimes for you.

---

## Crafting Materials

- **Super Slime** — the core material for almost everything in the mod. Also repairs slime tools.
- **Explosive Slime** — made by combining Super Slime and TNT. Nine of them can be crafted back from an Explosive Slime Block.
- **Explosive Slime Stick** — a crafting component for the Slime Scythe and Nether Scythe
- **End Battery** and **Nether Battery** — crafting components for the Mega Slime Grenade, Slime Scythe, and Nether Scythe

## Slime Blocks & Spawner

- **Explosive Slime Block** — a block that behaves like TNT (set it off with fire or redstone). It breaks instantly.
- **Slime Spawner** — a block you craft with iron blocks, a slime block, and a vanilla spawner. Right-click it with Super Slime to add fuel (up to 99, each worth one minute of spawning). Right-click it empty-handed to start or stop it. While running, it releases a small, medium, or large slime every 5 seconds.

## Tools & Weapons

- **Slime Sword**, **Slime Axe**, **Slime Pickaxe**, **Slime Shovel**, and **Slime Hoe** — tools with near-unbreakable durability that are repaired with Super Slime. The Slime Sword adds a huge amount of attack damage.
- **Slime Scythe** — a melee weapon that deals massive damage per hit
- **Nether Scythe** — a melee weapon with extended reach that also hits every living mob within 8 blocks of you with each strike
- **Kill Stick** — instantly kills whatever it hits
- **Rage Stick** — right-click one mob, then right-click a second one, and the two will start attacking each other

## Armor

- **Slime Armor** (Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots) — durable armor with netherite-level protection and toughness
  - **Chestplate** — lets you glide, like an elytra
  - **Boots** — bounce when you land (unless you're sneaking) and block fall damage
  - **Full set** — blocks damage from flying into walls and gives strong knockback resistance. Each piece you wear adds knockback resistance.
- **Slime Horse Armor** — protects your horse with strong knockback resistance while you ride it

## Throwables

- **Slime Grenade** — sticks to whatever it hits, then explodes after 3 seconds with a TNT-sized blast
- **Mega Slime Grenade** — sticks to whatever it hits, then explodes after 10 seconds with a huge blast that also destroys nearby obsidian

## Mobs

- **Explosive Slime Cube** — a slime that swells up when it gets close to you, then explodes. Larger cubes make bigger blasts.
- **Prince Slime** — a 100-health boss slime. When defeated, it splits into 3 large slimes and drops 5–10 Super Slime.
- **King Slime** — a 400-health boss slime. When defeated, it splits into 3 Prince Slimes and drops a pile of Super Slime, sometimes Explosive Slime, and rarely a grenade.

Prince and King Slimes show up during Slime Rain.

## Potions & Effects

- **Potion of Slime Luck** — for 5 minutes, breaking blocks and killing mobs have a 50% chance to drop an extra Super Slime
- **Potion of Slimy Misfortune** — applies Very Sticky for 5 seconds. Survive it to start a Slime Rain.
- **Knockback Resist** — gained from wearing Slime Armor. Stacks with the number of pieces you wear.
- **Sticky** — roots you in place. **Slime Arrows** (tipped arrows made with Super Slime) apply it for 40 seconds.
- **Very Sticky** — roots you in place and deals magic damage over time

## Dimension Pearls

Teleport to other dimensions without building a portal. Each pearl is used up on use.

- **Slime Teleporter Pearl** — sends you to the **Slime Dimension**, a flat world built from slime blocks
- **Overworld Teleporter Pearl** — sends you to the Overworld
- **Nether Teleporter Pearl** — sends you to the Nether
- **End Teleporter Pearl** — sends you to The End

## Slime Rain

Survive a Potion of Slimy Misfortune and a **Slime Rain** begins. It lasts 20 minutes, the weather turns to rain, and green slime falls from the sky. A Prince Slime appears about 40 blocks from each player, and there is a 10% chance a King Slime appears too. Surviving the storm earns an advancement.

## Advancements

The mod adds 20 advancements that guide you from your first Super Slime through the dimension pearls, slime armor, grenades, the Slime Spawner, and surviving a Slime Rain.
